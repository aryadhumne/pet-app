⚠️ Top Causes of Street Animal Deaths
🚗 Road Accidents — #1 Cause
Over 60% of street animal deaths in India occur due to vehicle accidents, especially at night on highways and city roads.
🦠 Disease Outbreaks
Canine Distemper, Parvovirus, FMD (cattle), and Mange are the leading infectious causes of death among street animals.
🦠 Disease Awareness & Prevention
Canine Distemper Virus (CDV)
Highly contagious viral illness in dogs. Spreads via airborne exposure. Symptoms: fever, discharge, seizures. Fatal if untreated.
🐕 Dogs — High Risk
✅ Prevention: Annual CDV vaccination. Avoid contact with unknown animals.
Foot & Mouth Disease (FMD)
Viral disease in cattle and pigs. Causes blisters on mouth and feet. Street cattle are especially vulnerable without vaccination.
🐄 Cattle — High Risk
✅ Report sightings to your local Pashupalan Department.
Mange (Sarcoptic)
Skin disease caused by mites. Extremely common in street dogs. Causes severe itching, hair loss, and secondary infections.
🐕 Dogs — Very Common
✅ Treatable with vet-prescribed ivermectin. Help a street dog today!
